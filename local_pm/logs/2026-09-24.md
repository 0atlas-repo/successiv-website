    `1.00 0.99 0.79 0.00 0.00 0.00 0.00`, and all 1.00 by about 4s.
  - With the reduced-motion block forced on (browse's CDP allowlist blocks media
    emulation, so the built CSS was rewritten temporarily and then restored),
    every step is at 1.00 with `animation-name: none` at 120ms.
  - Screenshots, mid-sequence and settled, in light and dark plus the card, are
    at `/private/tmp/mockanim/`.
