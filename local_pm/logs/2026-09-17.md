## What changed

Built the product-led Successiv site end to end, on a branch worktree at
`.worktrees/site-build`.

Discovered mid-task that a previous version already existed at
`/Users/r/Workspaces/0atlas/successiv.com/` — a shipped static site, an Astro
rebuild in progress, official logo SVGs, and a `previews/` folder with three

scratch" to "carry the existing brand forward onto the new positioning".

## Decisions

- Brief wins on content (product-led), existing site wins on brand. The brief
  records a founder lock, so it supersedes the old services framing.
- Astro over Next.js. `docs/BUILD.md` only suggested Next.js, and the existing
  rebuild was already Astro with the tokens done.
- Both themes, not one. Dark is the brand default; light is derived, because
  cyan #5acaed on white is ~1.9:1 and fails AA.
- 1line.ai ships as a fifth product on founder sign-off, with placeholder copy
  that admits it is placeholder.
- Hong Kong was not carried over — the founder left it unchecked.
- The old stat-card work design was dropped. Its own source comment warned
  against invented metrics and we have none.
