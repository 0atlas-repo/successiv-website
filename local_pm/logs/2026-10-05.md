## Theme switch: root cause found, colour morph shipped on `menu`

- Founder's 10s capture: the View Transition snapshot dropped the header and
  drew the 3D wall smaller and flatter. View Transitions abandoned.
- Root cause of every choppy colour fade: the "not inside the wall" selector
  excluded the runway's children but not the runway itself, so its inherited
  text colour faded and all wall elements restyled every frame.
  Fix: `*:not(.wall-runway, .wall-runway *)`.
- Wall: columns 0 and 1 (always behind the copy's scrim, 0% visible right of
  "Browse the work" up to 1920; 14-35% at 2560-3000) emptied at the founder's

  same as an instant switch (2); the one slow frame is the flip at ~0.27s, under
  the softened wall. An earlier run read 9 slow frames; re-run side by side, it
