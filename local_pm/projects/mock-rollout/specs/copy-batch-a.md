## Creators Sphere

**Positioning:** the AI checks the post; the brand only chooses who takes the gig.

- **One-liner:** Paid brand campaigns for creators: find the work, post it, prove it.
  *(was "…get paid". There is no payout or balance code; "Paid" is never written.)*
- **Problem:** Creator deals still run on DMs and agency threads. A brand can't
  easily tell whether the post that went up is the one it paid for.
- **Our solution:** Brands post campaigns with the real brief. Creators apply,
  publish and submit the live post, and the AI checks it against the brief. The
  brand only decides who takes each gig.

| # | Title | Body | Screen (phone) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find a campaign | Browse live campaigns by category, each with its pay, deadline and brief. | Explore: chips, cards fill in, a chip re-filters |
| 2 | Apply, and the brand picks | Apply with one tap. The brand approves who takes each slot. | Campaign details: "Apply now" → "Pending approval" |
| 3 | Post it, then submit it | Publish on your own Instagram, then pick the post in the app. | "Select Instagram Post" strip, "Submit proof" |
| 4 | The AI checks the post | It reads the image and caption against the brief, or against the approved draft, and marks the proof within minutes. | "Proof under review" → "Proof verified" |
| 5 | Track every gig | One list shows what needs you next. | "Action needed" chips, cards with Do it / Waiting / Open |

Retired claims (verify check 10): pending/available balance, reward moves to
balance, slot released to the next creator, hashtag/mention check, creator

## Shopmgr

**Positioning:** the assistant answers on its own; a person handles only the
tickets it raises and the gaps behind them.

- **One-liner (kept):** Store chat that answers from your catalogue and
  policies — and escalates when it should.
- **Problem (kept):** Storefront chat handles the easy questions and stalls on the
  ones that decide a sale: stock, variants, delivery, returns. Shoppers leave;
  staff never see the thread.
- **Our solution:** The assistant answers shoppers from your real catalogue and
  policies, and raises a ticket when it can't. Your staff handle the tickets and
  fill the gaps.

| # | Title | Body | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Switch it on in Shopify | Turn on the app embed in your theme. The catalogue syncs by itself. | Admin welcome → embed toggle on → "…successfully activated." |
| 2 | It answers from your catalogue | Each reply comes from your products and policies, found by meaning, not keywords. | Widget: question → typing → answer → "Highlight N considered products" |
| 3 | It raises a ticket instead of guessing | A refund, return or cancellation, or anything it can't answer, becomes a ticket. | Same chat: out-of-policy ask → ticket raised, typed |
| 4 | Human in the loop | Staff pick up the ticket in the portal, add a remark and close it. | Portal Support Ticket: new ticket Open → remark → Closed |
| 5 | Close the gap | Add the missing guide or policy, and the assistant uses it once indexed. | Portal Knowledge: "Add New Knowledge" → Pending → Success |

Retired claims: staff review and rate conversations. The home hero keeps its
current chat drawing; step 2 gets its own mock.

## KYC — needs a decision before any copy

The shipped generation has none of: a reviewer, a one-time code, expiry, or an
event log. It marks a holder's whole profile "verified" after any one saved
document, with no check. What is real: holders keep documents in a phone app; a
counterparty asks on the web with a QR code; the holder accepts or declines.

**Option 1 — describe what shipped.** Positioning: nothing moves without the
holder's yes.
- **One-liner:** Hold identity documents once, and share them only when asked.
- **Problem:** The same passport and proof of address get emailed to a dozen
  counterparties, and every one of them keeps a copy.
- **Our solution:** A holder keeps their documents in one app. A counterparty asks
  for exactly what it needs with a QR code, and nothing is shared until the holder
  accepts.

| # | Title | Body | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep your documents in one place | Passport, ID card, address and phone number, kept by the holder in the phone app. | phone Home list, no status pills |
| 2 | Add a document | Type in the details and photograph the front and back. | phone passport form → Save |
| 3 | A counterparty asks | On the web it ticks exactly the documents it needs, and gets a QR code for the holder to scan. | web request form → Submit → QR code |
| 4 | Human in the loop | The holder sees the request and accepts or declines it. Nothing is shared until they accept. | phone "Accept Information Sharing" dialog |
| 5 | The counterparty sees the answer | The shared details appear in its portal, request by request. | web portal list → detail, no "verified" row |
