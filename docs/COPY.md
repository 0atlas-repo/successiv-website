## Home — how we work

We build software that has been tested in the enterprise, and is running there — not a chat wrapper, and not a demo that falls apart in production.

1. Business success is the goal. Automation and transformation are how we get there. We measure the work after go-live, not a model score.
2. Build against a real pain point. We start from the problem in the work, and we connect the software to the systems you already run. A process that never reaches the books does not help anyone.
3. You talk to the people doing the work.

## Home — products we ship

KYC and Accounting first. Shopmgr and Creators Sphere under “Also in production.”

- **KYC** — Hold identity documents once, and share them only when asked. A holder keeps their documents in one app. A counterparty asks for exactly what it needs with a QR code, and nothing is shared until the holder accepts.
- **Accounting** (pre-release) — The system reads the document; a person still answers the awkward question. The AI reads each bill, drafts the double entry, checks its own work, and redoes it when the check fails. You answer the odd unclear figure and approve the result.
- **Shopmgr** — Store chat that answers from your catalogue and policies — and escalates when it should.
- **Creators Sphere** — Paid brand campaigns for creators: find the work, post it, prove it.
